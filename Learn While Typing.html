Mathematics

Fractions

A fraction represents part of a whole. Its numerator shows the parts we have, while its denominator shows the equal parts in the whole.

Patterns

A number pattern follows a rule. Looking for the change between terms can help us predict the next number with confidence.

Geometry

A triangle has three sides, and the angles inside any triangle add up to one hundred and eighty degrees.

Percentages

A percentage means a part out of one hundred. It can describe discounts, test results, and changes in a quantity.

Science

The Water Cycle

Heat from the Sun causes water to evaporate. The water vapour cools into clouds and returns to Earth as precipitation.

Forces

A force is a push or a pull. Forces can change an object's speed, direction, or shape.

Plant Life

Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make food through a process called photosynthesis.

The Solar System

The solar system contains the Sun and the objects that orbit it, including planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

English Literature

Character

A character is a person, animal, or figure in a story. Writers reveal character through actions, speech, thoughts, and relationships.

Setting

The setting tells us where and when a story happens. It can shape the mood and influence the choices of the characters.

Poetry

Poets use rhythm, imagery, and carefully chosen words to express ideas and feelings in a concentrated form.

Theme

A theme is a central idea in a work of literature, such as friendship, courage, justice, or the search for identity.

History

Evidence

Historians study sources such as letters, tools, photographs, and buildings to learn about people who lived in the past.

Ancient Civilisations

Ancient civilisations developed systems of writing, government, trade, farming, and architecture that shaped later societies.

Cause and Effect

Historical events usually have several causes and consequences. Comparing them helps us understand change over time.

Chronology

Chronology is the arrangement of events in the order they happened. Timelines make relationships between events easier to see.

General Knowledge

Maps

Maps use symbols, scale, and direction to represent places. A compass helps us describe the position of one place from another.

Communication

Communication allows people to share information through speech, writing, images, gestures, and technology.

Healthy Habits

Regular movement, balanced meals, enough sleep, and good hygiene help the body and mind work well.

Digital Citizenship

Good digital citizens protect private information, check sources, and treat other people with respect online.

Geography

Landforms

Mountains, valleys, rivers, and plains are landforms that shape the surface of Earth.

Computer Science

Algorithms

An algorithm is a clear sequence of instructions used to solve a problem or complete a task.

Art

Colour

Artists use colour to create contrast, mood, balance, and emphasis in an artwork.

Music

Rhythm

Rhythm is the pattern of beats and pauses that gives music its movement through time.

Languages

Vocabulary

Vocabulary is the collection of words we understand and use to communicate ideas clearly.

Health

Wellbeing

Wellbeing includes caring for physical health, mental health, relationships, and daily habits.
